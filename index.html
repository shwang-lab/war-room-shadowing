IDIOM / FIGURATIVE USE

Live pronunciation scoring needs Chrome (desktop or Android). You can still play the reference audio and record yourself, but automatic scoring isn't available in this browser.
– match to target sentence
Reference audio is computer speech (browser text-to-speech), voiced male or female to roughly match who says the line in the film — not the actors' own voices. Pronunciation scoring compares what the browser's speech recognizer hears you say against the target sentence (a text-match estimate, not a phonetic analysis), so treat the percentage as a helpful guide rather than an exact grade. Context notes marked word-overlap or manual in the data are close paraphrases rather than exact film quotes; a few entries with no confirmed scene are labeled General / Theme.
